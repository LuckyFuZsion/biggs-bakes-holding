## 2. What we collect

**Mailing list sign-up.** When you sign up on our holding page or website we collect:
- your email address;
- the wording you agreed to and the date and time you signed up (so we can prove consent);
- which page you signed up on;
- the date and time you confirmed your address, or unsubscribed;
- a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used only to stop abuse and automated sign-ups. We cannot turn it back into your IP address.

**Orders.** When you place an order we collect your name, delivery address, email address, phone number if you give one, and the contents of your order. Payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your card details.

**Getting in touch.** If you contact us, we keep your message and your contact details so we can reply.

**Website use.** Our sign-up form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot check, which processes technical information from your browser to tell people from automated scripts. [If you add analytics or advertising cookies, describe them here and add a cookie banner.]

## 3. Why we use it and our legal basis

| What we do | Why | Legal basis (UK GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Send you emails about new flavours and drops | You asked us to | **Consent** (you can withdraw it at any time) |
| Send a confirmation email when you sign up | To check the address is yours | Consent |

We will only email you about new flavours, drops and offers if you have signed up and confirmed your address. We use a double opt-in: after you sign up we email you a link, and you are only added to the list once you press the confirmation button.

## 4. Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use trusted companies to run the service on our behalf. They may only use your data for that purpose:

- **Cloudflare**: hosting, security and our sign-up service, plus the Turnstile bot check.
- **Google (Firebase)**: the database that stores our mailing list and website content.
- **Resend**: sends our confirmation and marketing emails.
- **Shopify**: runs our online shop, orders and payments.
- **Vercel**: hosts our main website.
- **Delivery couriers**: receive your name, address and phone number to deliver your order.
- **Professional advisers and authorities**: accountants, lawyers or regulators where we are required to.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or an adequacy decision, so your data remains protected.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:
- be told what data we hold about you and get a copy;
- have wrong information corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict or object to how we use it;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- **withdraw your consent to marketing at any time.** Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us.

To use any of these rights, email hello@biggsbakes.com. We will reply within one month.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, restricted access (only authorised people can see the mailing list, and admin access needs two-step verification), bot protection and rate limiting. No system is completely secure, but we take care to keep your information safe.
